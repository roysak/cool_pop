How to Play

Quick taps. Cool pops. Beat your best!

  • Pop the cool balloons

    Tap or click before they disappear. Faster pops earn more points, and consecutive pops build your combo.

  • Let red float by

    Popping red costs one life and resets your combo. You start with 3 lives; the round ends when they run out.

  • Rainbow = double points

    Rainbow balloons double the points for that pop.

  • 🐌
    White snail = slow down

    Pop a snail balloon to ease the pace as the game speeds up.

  • ♥
    White heart = extra life

    Restore one life, up to 3. Missing a heart has no penalty.

Missing a cool balloon resets your combo. For an extra challenge, enable Hard mode in the pause menu: misses also cost a life.
